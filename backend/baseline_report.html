Why the Baseline Fails

🔐
Shared Credentials
Multiple employees share one login. When a sensitive action is logged, only the shared account name is recorded.
⚠️
Zero Individual Accountability
IAR = 0%. It is impossible to determine which person approved a payment or deleted a record.
📊
Audit Gap
78 HIGH/CRITICAL actions are attributed only to a shared account. Investigators cannot identify the responsible individual.
